Allocation Automation

End-to-end automation solution developed to reduce manual allocation effort and improve operational efficiency within Accounts Payable processes.

Business Challenge

Allocation activities required repetitive manual processing, validation checks and data handling, resulting in significant operational effort and potential inconsistencies.

Impact

  • Reduced manual allocation effort by approximately 220 hours annually
  • Improved process consistency and allocation accuracy
  • Reduced repetitive manual processing activities
  • Improved scalability for future operational growth

Key Learning

Successful automation requires more than coding. Business rule validation, stakeholder alignment, testing and process governance are equally important to achieving sustainable improvements.
