Digital Governance

Digital Tool Lifecycle Management

Governance initiative focused on improving visibility, ownership, review and lifecycle control of digital solutions and project records.

Business Challenge

As digital tools grow across the organisation, visibility, ownership and review processes become increasingly difficult. Without governance, tools risk becoming outdated, duplicated, or unsupported.

My Role

  • Project Lead
  • Business Analyst
  • Solution Designer
  • Governance Process Owner
  • Stakeholder Engagement

Solution Approach

Project Registration
↓
Ownership Validation
↓
Periodic Review
↓
Exception Detection
↓
Management Approval
↓
Lifecycle Closure

Technologies Used

Power Apps Dataverse SharePoint Power Automate

Impact

  • Improves governance visibility
  • Promotes accountable ownership
  • Supports lifecycle reviews
  • Reduces risk of unmanaged solutions

Key Learning

Digital transformation requires governance and ownership in addition to technology. Sustainable solutions depend on clear accountability, review processes and lifecycle management.
